STRIKELINE

Counter squad vs. raiders, 4 against 4, first team to 7 rounds wins. You lead the blue squad with 3 bot teammates; clear every raider before the clock runs out.

Touch: left stick moves, drag the right side to aim, ✹ fires (hold), ⤒ jumps, ↻ reloads, ⇄ swaps weapon, ◎ scopes the Longshot.
Keyboard: WASD, Space jump, F fire, R reload, 1–3 or Q swap weapons. With touch controls off, the mouse aims, left-click fires and right-click scopes.
